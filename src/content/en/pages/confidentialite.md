This site collects as little data as possible. It uses no advertising cookies, no third-party trackers and no analytics tool that follows you from one site to another.

The data controller is Nicolas Maigre, who can be reached through the [contact form](/contact).

If you have received a personal link, I am notified by email the first time you open it. These notifications are sorted and deleted every quarter. The emails I send contain no tracking pixels.

A message sent through the contact form stays on the site's server only until it is forwarded to my mailbox, and is deleted from the server within 7 days. To limit abuse, messages sent from the same connection are counted for one hour, in memory only and without keeping the IP address.

This data is neither sold nor passed on to third parties, apart from the technical providers listed below. Only the site's publisher has access to it. Tools that help me organise my correspondence may see a contact's name and organisation, but never their personal link.

Encrypted server backups are kept for no more than 3 months.

## Where the data is held

The server is located in Canada and operated by OVH. Canada is covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission, which recognises a level of protection equivalent to that of the European Union. Traffic passes through Cloudflare, which acts as a technical intermediary to protect the site and make it load faster.

Two providers therefore process data on the site's behalf: Cloudflare (DNS, protection and delivery of the site) and OVHcloud (server hosting, and the mailbox that receives messages from the contact form).

## Cookies

The site sets only one cookie, and only if you open a personal link: `__Host-espace`, a session cookie that is strictly necessary to access the private area and is deleted when it expires. Cookies essential for authentication are exempt from consent (Article 82 of the French Data Protection Act, known as the loi Informatique et Libertés, and the CNIL guidelines of 17 September 2020), so no cookie banner is shown to you.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, rectify or erase your data, or object to its processing, through the [contact form](/contact). If you are not satisfied with the response, you can lodge a complaint with the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/plaintes), the French data protection authority.
