This site collects as little data as possible. It uses no advertising cookies, no third-party trackers and no analytics tool that follows you from one site to another.

The data controller is Nicolas M, who can be reached through the [contact form](/contact).

If you have received a personal link, I am notified by email the first time you open it, then at most once a day if someone tries to use it after it has expired or been revoked. These notifications are sorted and deleted every quarter. The emails I send contain no tracking pixels.

A message sent through the contact form is kept on the site's server until it is forwarded to my mailbox, then deleted no more than 8 days later. To limit abuse, messages sent from the same connection are counted for one hour, in memory only and without keeping the IP address.

This data is neither sold nor passed on to third parties, apart from the technical providers listed below. Only the site's publisher has access to it. Tools that help me organise my correspondence may see a contact's name and organisation, but never their personal link.

Encrypted server backups are kept for no more than 3 months.

The game [Frontfall](https://nclsmgr.com/frontfall/) can be played as a guest, and does not record games: they only exist in memory. Signing in with Discord, Google or a link sent by email is optional; it then keeps your name, a scrambled id, the way you signed in and your achievements, never your email in clear, until you delete the account from the game's menu. The sign-in email goes out through the site's mailbox at OVH, and the address is forgotten once it is sent. The details are in the [privacy policy of the game](https://nclsmgr.com/frontfall/privacy.html). To limit abuse, it counts the connections opened from the same IP address, in memory only. Its logs never record that address in the clear, only a fingerprint that changes every time the game restarts; they are kept for 30 days, like the server's.

## Where the data is held

The server is located in France and operated by OVH. Traffic passes through Cloudflare, which acts as a technical intermediary to protect the site and make it load faster.

Two providers therefore process data on the site's behalf: Cloudflare (DNS, protection and delivery of the site) and OVHcloud (server hosting, and the mailbox that receives messages from the contact form).

## Audience measurement

Audience measurement uses Umami, installed on the site's own server: no data is passed to a third party. Umami sets no cookies and does not store your IP address. It only uses it during the visit to work out your country, then discards it. Statistics are kept for 13 months.

Nothing is measured in the private area. If your browser sends the Do Not Track signal, your visit is not counted.

## Cookies

The site sets only one cookie, and only if you open a personal link: `__Host-espace`, a session cookie that is strictly necessary to access the private area and is deleted when it expires. The game Frontfall keeps a token in the browser's session storage so that you can take your seat back after a disconnection; it disappears when you close the tab. It also keeps, in the browser's local storage, the language you chose and your last game settings, nickname included, until you clear the site's data. These strictly necessary trackers are exempt from consent (Article 82 of the French Data Protection Act, known as the loi Informatique et Libertés, and the CNIL guidelines of 17 September 2020), so no cookie banner is shown to you.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, rectify or erase your data, or object to its processing, through the [contact form](/contact). If you are not satisfied with the response, you can lodge a complaint with the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/plaintes), the French data protection authority.
